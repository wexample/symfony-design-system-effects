Effects of the wexample brand for the design system: generated avatars (wexies-2020) first

## Table of Contents

- [Installation](#installation)
- [Introduction](#introduction)

## Installation

Require the package and register its bundle in `config/bundles.php`, after the design system's:

```php
Wexample\SymfonyDesignSystemEffects\WexampleSymfonyDesignSystemEffectsBundle::class => ['all' => true],
```

Nothing to configure: its avatar engine joins the design system's on its own.

Effects of the wexample brand for the design system, kept out of it so it stays plain: each one plugs into a seam `symfony-design-system` opens. Avatar engines first: `wexies-2020`, the faces network gave its members from 2019 on, and `facets`, planes of colour crossing on white.
